Accessibility Statement

Poster Suite is committed to making communication accessible for everyone. Both our tools and this Hub are built with the following standards in mind:

  • Keyboard Navigable: Every feature can be accessed using `Tab`, `Enter`, and `Escape` without a mouse.
  • Screen-Reader Optimized: Interactive elements use proper ARIA roles and labels.
  • Sensory-Friendly: A built-in "Calm Mode" reduces visual noise, animations, and harsh contrasts.
  • No Tracking: We do not use cookies or external analytics that might slow down assistive technologies.

About Poster Suite

Poster Suite is born from a personal passion for creating free, open-source tools that make communication more accessible and design simpler for everyone.

Both PosterA11y and PosterD45h are designed to run entirely in your browser—no accounts, no subscriptions, and no data tracking. It's just pure, accessible tech built to help care homes, support workers, and families communicate better.

If you find these tools helpful, feel free to explore the code, fork it, and share it with anyone who might benefit.

Quick Tips for Accessible Design

Want to make sure your posters are easy for everyone to read? Follow these 5 golden rules:

  • Keep it short: Aim for no more than 15 words per line or block.
  • Use clear fonts: Stick to sans-serif fonts like Lexend or Atkinson Hyperlegible.
  • Left-align text: Centered text is hard for the eye to track. Left-align whenever possible.
  • High contrast: Ensure dark text sits on a light background, or vice versa.
  • Support with images: Use symbols or simple icons to explain key words.
